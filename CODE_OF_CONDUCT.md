# Code of Conduct

LiveQuery is a respectful, welcoming open source project. This policy adopts
Laravel's emphasis on good-faith discussion, respectful disagreement, no personal
attacks and no harassment.

- Assume good intent and engage constructively with differences of opinion.
- No harassment, threats, personal attacks, discrimination or degrading comments.
- Respect users' privacy, intellectual property and security reports.
- Maintainers may moderate or remove harmful content and restrict participation.

**Reporting:** Before making the repository public, the owner must configure a
private maintainer contact for confidential conduct reports. Until then, do not
use public issues to report private or sensitive matters.

References: https://laravel.com/docs/13.x/contributions
